Quick Sheet – Support

How to use it

  1. Open any workbook in Excel.
  2. On the Home tab, in the Quick Sheet group, click New Sheet. A sheet called “New Sheet” is added at the end (numbered if that name is taken).
  3. To choose the name, click Named Sheet, type a name in the pane and click Create sheet.
