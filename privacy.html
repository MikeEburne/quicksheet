Quick Sheet – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Quick Sheet is an Excel add-in published by Mantis Networks (Pty) Ltd (“we”).

What the add-in does with your data

Quick Sheet runs entirely inside Excel. It adds a worksheet and writes a short header in cell A1. It does not read the contents of your workbook, and it does not send your data, your workbook or any personal information to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or share personal information through the add-in. We do not use cookies or analytics in the add-in.

Microsoft may collect usage data about add-ins under its own privacy statement when you install them from Microsoft AppSource.

Your rights (POPIA)

Because we hold no personal information from the add-in, there is nothing to access or delete. If you have questions, contact our Information Officer at privacy@mantisnetworks.co.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
